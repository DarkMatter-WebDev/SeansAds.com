Google Ads structure for Naples Estate Jewelry

  • Campaign groups: sell estate jewelry Naples, sell gold Naples, sterling silver buyers, luxury watch buyers, antiques and estate liquidation, and inherited collection evaluations.
  • Landing page alignment: match ads to the exact asset category, then reinforce private service, immediate payment, mobile appointments, and direct call/text access.
  • Negative keyword cleanup: filter repair, retail shopping, free appraisal-only, costume jewelry, low-value pawn traffic, and unrelated antique browsing searches.
  • Tracking notes: phone calls, photo submissions, consultation requests, schedule clicks, and estate-services inquiries.
Best ad-message themes

The strongest message is local, private, and fast: a Naples-based buyer who comes to the client, evaluates estate assets discreetly, and can provide immediate payment when an offer is accepted.

Covenant Jewelry Buyers

Covenant Jewelry Buyers is a Naples, Florida business focused on acquiring high-value personal and estate assets. Their public site positions the company around private evaluations, transparent market-based assessments, immediate purchasing capability, and confidential handling for individual sellers, estates, and advisors.

  • Primary categories: estate jewelry, fine jewelry, sterling silver, gold, coins, bullion, designer pieces, and luxury Swiss watches.
  • Estate services include consultation, professional valuation, settlement/reporting documentation, direct purchase, auction consignment, and private-sale placement.
  • Buyer intent is strongly local and action-driven: call, request a private evaluation, visit the Naples office, or discuss estate coordination.
  • Team signal: Sean Cochrane is listed with a focus on digital marketing and day-to-day operations.
